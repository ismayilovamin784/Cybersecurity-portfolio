## 📝 Final statement

> My name is Amin. I got into cybersecurity after my gaming account and, later, my mom's social media account were hacked. I saw how stressful that is for a person, and I want to help stop it from happening to others. I'm curious, I like learning how systems work and how to protect them, and I keep building my skills one step at a time.

## 💪 Strengths

1. **Curiosity.** When I find something interesting, I keep digging until I understand how it works. That's how I got into cybersecurity in the first place.
2. **Persistence.** I finished all nine courses of the Google Cybersecurity Certificate and earned my OPSWAT certificate, and I did it by learning a little at a time instead of rushing.
3. **Empathy for victims.** I've been on the receiving end of a hack, and I've seen it happen to my mom. I know security problems aren't just technical, they hit real people.

## 🧭 Values

1. **Protecting people.** Nobody should lose something that matters to them because of one weak password or one fake link.
2. **Honesty and responsibility.** If someone trusts you with their data, you take that seriously and you don't cut corners.

## ❓ Clarifying questions

**What most interests me about the field of cybersecurity?**
I'm fascinated by how an attack really works, step by step, and what would have stopped it. Social engineering stands out to me, because it shows that attackers often trick people instead of breaking technology.

**Who is the audience for my professional statement?**
Right now, university admissions committees, since this portfolio is part of my application. In the future, cybersecurity recruiters and employers.

**In what ways can my strengths, values, and interest in cybersecurity support the security goals of various organizations?**
Every organization wants to keep its data and its people safe. I bring curiosity to notice what could go wrong, persistence to keep learning in a field that changes quickly, and a personal reason to take security seriously. I'm still at the beginning, but I'm building the skills to back that up.
