# Hands-on activities

Practical activities from the Google Cybersecurity Certificate, all completed. Activities marked ⭐ are the course's **portfolio activities**. Company scenarios, such as Botium Toys, are fictional and used for practice.

| # | Activity | What it shows | Course | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Professional Statement](professional-statement/) | Strengths, values and a short personal statement | Course 1 | ⭐ Portfolio activity |
| 2 | [Security Audit: Botium Toys](botium-toys-security-audit/) | Risk assessment, controls and compliance checklist | Course 2 | Hands-on activity |
| 3 | [Cybersecurity Incident Report: SYN Flood](network-attack-analysis-syn-flood/) | Identifying a DoS attack and how the TCP handshake is abused | Course 3 | Hands-on activity |
| 4 | [Network Traffic Analysis: DNS and ICMP](network-traffic-analysis-dns-icmp/) | Reading a tcpdump log to find why a site is unreachable | Course 3 | Hands-on activity |
| 5 | [Security Incident Report: Brute Force and Malware](security-incident-report-brute-force/) | Documenting an incident from a packet capture, with a 2FA fix | Course 3 | Hands-on activity |
| 6 | [Security Risk Assessment: Network Hardening](security-risk-assessment-hardening/) | Choosing firewall, password and MFA hardening measures | Course 3 | Hands-on activity |
| 7 | [Incident Report Analysis: NIST CSF](incident-report-analysis-nist-csf/) | Applying the five NIST CSF functions to an ICMP flood | Course 3 | ⭐ Portfolio activity |
