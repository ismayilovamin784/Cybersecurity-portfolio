## Big picture
The last course is about the next step: turning the skills from the certificate into a job search. It shows how AI tools can help with planning, applications and interview practice.

## What it covers
| Area | What the course describes |
|---|---|
| Finding direction | Discover skills and explore career options with a tool called Career Dreamer |
| Staying organized | Track applications with Google Sheets |
| Resume and plan | Build a resume and a step-by-step job search plan with help from Gemini |
| Interview practice | Practice answering questions with Gemini Notebook and Gemini Live |
| Online presence | Build a personal brand and web presence |

## What I learned
The main lesson was that a job search is a project, so it needs a plan, a way to track progress and regular practice. AI tools can make each step faster, but my skills and my own voice still have to come through.
